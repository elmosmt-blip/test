Section routing rules for SMTInsider publication. Use these guidelines to decide the editorial section:

- news: company appointments, acquisitions, facility openings, financial reports, awards, partnerships
- insight: how-to guides, troubleshooting, process control explanations, checklists, technical deep-dives
- review: specific equipment/platform/series reviews, buyer guides, comparisons, demo/adoption questions
- vendor: vendor/supplier/company profiles

When in doubt, prefer insight for technical process content and news for company/event announcements.

Return a JSON object with:
{
  "editorial_type": "news | insight | review | vendor",
  "section_path": "/news/ | /insights/ | /reviews/ | /vendors/",
  "confidence": 0.0-1.0,
  "reasons": ["reason1", "reason2"]
}
